## Social Networks and the Decision to Insure

ECON 481 project. The notebook `project.ipynb` replicates and extends:

* Cai, J., de Janvry, A., & Sadoulet, E. (2015). [Social Networks and the Decision to Insure](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.20130442). *American Economic Journal: Applied Economics*, 7(2), 81–108.

Rice farmers in Jiangxi, China, were offered a new weather insurance product in two rounds of information sessions, with randomized intensive sessions and randomized default options. We estimate the peer effect of village take-up on a farmer's own take-up by 2SLS, instrumenting first-round take-up with the randomized default,.

### Running

```bash
conda env create -f environment.yml
conda activate projects-student-template
jupyter lab project.ipynb
```
